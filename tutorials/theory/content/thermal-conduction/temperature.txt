<p>
Temperature is a measure of the kinetic energy of particles in a material:
<ul>
<li>Atoms and molecules in a solid or fluid move or vibrate</li>
<li>The faster they move the higher the temperature</li>
<li>Temperature differences drive heat flow from hot regions to cold regions</li>
</ul>
</p>

<p>
In this modelling \(\theta(x)\) is the temperature difference relative to the surroundings representing the energy available to flow along or out of the rod:
</p>

<p>
$$
\theta(x) = T(x) - T_\infty
$$
</p>

<p>
<ul>
<li>\(T(x)\) - actual temperature at position \(x\) along the rod</li>
<li>\(T_\infty\) - temperature of the surroundings</li>
<li>\(\theta(x)\) - temperature difference</li>
</ul>
</p>
