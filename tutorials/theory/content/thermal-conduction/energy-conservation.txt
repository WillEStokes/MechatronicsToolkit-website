<p>
Energy cannot be created or destroyed so the total energy of a closed system remains constant. We consider an energy balance on a small section of the rod which is in steady state:
</p>

<p>
$$
\text{Energy In} = \text{Energy Out}
$$
</p>

<p>
The rod segment has length \(dx\). Heat enters at position \(x\), leaves at position \(x + dx\) and some heat transfers radially with surroundings:
